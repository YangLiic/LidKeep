# Community guidelines

Help make LidKeep's issues, pull requests and discussions useful and welcoming.

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels and opinions.
- Describe bugs and disagreements with concrete facts; focus criticism on code and behavior.
- Do not post harassment, threats, discriminatory insults or others' private information.
- Keep discussion relevant and allow maintainers time to respond.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close derailed discussions, or restrict participants when needed. Moderation should be proportionate and explain the reason where possible. For a sensitive report, request a private maintainer contact channel without publishing private details; a published repository should provide that channel before building a large community.

These guidelines apply to project-hosted discussions. They do not promise moderation of unrelated external spaces.
